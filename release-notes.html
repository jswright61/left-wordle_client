Release Notes

What's new in each release, most recent first.
Version numbers increase but aren't always sequential — a number is sometimes skipped before release.

v1.0.0

  • This release, with online play, is the official 1.0 release
  • The only updates since v0.9.21 are backend tooling

v0.9.22

  • Online accounts now sync in real time — every guess is pushed to the server as you play, replacing the old sync-on-login behavior. Fixes a bug where logging in on a second device with its own play history could silently wipe that history.
  • Starting a game on one logged-in device and then opening the app on another device now resumes the game in progress, instead of showing a stale or empty board.
  • The account icon now turns green while online (synced to your account), so you can tell at a glance that your play is being saved to the server.
  • If your online session expires or is revoked, you'll now get a one-time notice the next time you open the app, instead of quietly reverting to local-only play.
  • Fixed two device-link bugs: the "link this device" landing page could render invisible, and starting passkey registration while a device-link was pending could create a brand-new unlinked account instead of joining the one you meant to link.
  • The Stats screen now updates immediately after finishing a game or adjusting stats while logged in, instead of requiring a refresh.
  • Fixed a bug where a tab left open overnight could score a guess against the wrong day or incorrectly affect your streak; you'll now be warned and offered the choice to continue the stale puzzle or reload today's.
  • Newly created passkeys now correctly show a "last used" date right away instead of "Never."
  • Added an Online Accounts help page, linked from the Account menu, explaining how syncing across devices works.
  • Housekeeping: search-engine indexing rules and caching/deploy infrastructure updates — nothing else player-facing this release.

v0.9.20

  • Added this Release Notes page, plus a staging-only Things to Test page — both linked from Preferences and from the update-available prompt.
  • Housekeeping: deploy tooling fixes only, otherwise nothing else player-facing this release.

v0.9.19

  • Preferences, statistics, and game history for logged-in accounts now sync automatically as you play — not just when using Adjust Stats or Import.
  • New accounts push your local data to the server immediately on creation, and Restore from Backup now syncs the restored data too.
  • Failed syncs are retried automatically instead of being silently dropped.
  • Fixed a bug where changing time zones (e.g. after travel) could incorrectly break or preserve a streak.
  • Restore from Backup is now available directly from the Tools menu.
  • Redesigned the header icons — account moved to the right, tools moved next to the title — and improved the login-prompt flow.
  • New accounts no longer get a one-time "import this device's data" step; you're now warned up front and pointed to whichever device has your fullest history.
  • Renamed "Settings" to "Preferences" and "Signed In/Out" to "Logged In/Out" throughout the app.

v0.9.18

  • Deploy tooling fix only (SSH key support for deployments) — no player-facing changes this release.
