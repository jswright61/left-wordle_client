Start with a History in Your Browser / Device

If you don't have a history (or a significant history) on staging for you current browser / device, consider jumpstarting your staging history from the production site.

  1. Open Left Wordle (Production)
  2. Open the Tools modal (wrench icon in header)
  3. Under "Troubleshooting" click "Download All Settings"
  4. Open Left Wordle (Staging)
  5. This time, dismiss the login prompt without clicking "Don't ask me to log in" — we still want it to prompt you again for the Logins task below
  6. Open the Tools modal (same wrench icon, now on the left side of the header)
  7. Under "Backup & Restore" click "Restore from File"
  8. Select the file you just downloaded
  9. Click "Overwrite & Restore" to confirm
  10. Click the statistics (bar graph) icon in the header and verify the stats match your production stats
  11. Note: these stats are a point-in-time copy of production, not linked to it. Games played here will not be reflected on production.
  12. Refresh the page, or click the account icon to proceed with account testing.

Logins

You should be prompted to create a login. PLEASE create one and test various scenarios listed below. This is the biggest change yet for Left Wordle.

Logins use Passkeys — Passkey info here. Without an optional email, your login info on the server will not contain any personally identifiable information.

Signup on your first device

  1. In the login overlay, optionally enter an email address (optional — this is just for account recovery, not required)
  2. Optionally edit the device nickname field (it's pre-filled with a guessed name)
  3. Click "Create Passkey"
  4. Complete your device/browser's passkey prompt (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key)
  5. Verify the status message shows "Passkey created." and the overlay switches to the logged-in view
  6. Verify a brief on-screen message appears confirming your local data was saved to the new account
  7. Close the overlay and confirm the header's account icon now shows the logged-in state (tooltip: "Account (logged in)")

Login on your second device

  1. On your first device (now logged in), click the account icon to reopen the overlay
  2. Click "Add a Device (Link)"
  3. Verify the "Add a Device" modal opens with a one-time link (note: it expires soon and can only be used once)
  4. Copy the link (or use "Email Me a Link" instead) and open it on your second device
  5. On the second device, verify you land on a "Set up passkey" screen with a pre-filled device nickname
  6. Click "Set Up Passkey" and complete your device's passkey prompt
  7. Verify the page reloads and the account icon shows the logged-in state
  8. Click the statistics icon and verify the stats match the account from your first device
  9. Log out on the second device, then click "Log In" (not "Create Passkey") and verify you're signed back in using the passkey you just created

"Don't ask me to log in"

  1. On a logged-out device, when the login prompt appears on load, click "Don't ask me to log in" instead of signing up or logging in
  2. Verify the overlay closes
  3. Reload the page
  4. Verify the login prompt does NOT automatically reappear this time
  5. Click the account icon to open the overlay manually — verify the "Don't ask me to log in" link is no longer shown, but you can still create a passkey or log in from here
  6. Log in (or create a passkey) from this manual overlay
  7. Log out
  8. Reload the page again
  9. Verify the login prompt automatically reappears this time — logging in on a device resets the "don't ask" setting so a future logout doesn't leave you stranded on a signed-out device
