Start with a History in Your Browser / Device

If you don't have a history (or a significant history) on staging for you current browser / device, consider jumpstarting your staging history from the production site.

  1. Open Left Wordle (Production)
  2. Open the Tools modal (wrench icon in header)
  3. Under "Troubleshooting" click "Download All Settings"
  4. Open Left Wordle (Staging)
  5. This time, dismiss the login prompt without clicking "Don't ask me to log in" — we still want it to prompt you again for the Logins task below
  6. Open the Tools modal (same wrench icon, now on the left side of the header)
  7. Under "Backup & Restore" click "Restore from File"
  8. Select the file you just downloaded
  9. Click "Overwrite & Restore" to confirm
  10. Click the statistics (bar graph) icon in the header and verify the stats match your production stats
  11. Note: these stats are a point-in-time copy of production, not linked to it. Games played here will not be reflected on production.
  12. Refresh the page, or click the account icon to proceed with account testing.

Logins

You should be prompted to create a login. PLEASE create one and test various scenarios listed below. This is the biggest change yet for Left Wordle.

Logins use Passkeys — Passkey info here. Without an optional email, your login info on the server will not contain any personally identifiable information.

Signup on your first device

  1. In the login overlay, optionally enter an email address (optional — this is just for account recovery, not required)
  2. Optionally edit the device nickname field (it's pre-filled with a guessed name)
  3. Click "Create Passkey"
  4. Complete your device/browser's passkey prompt (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key)
  5. Verify the status message shows "Passkey created." and the overlay switches to the logged-in view
  6. Verify a brief on-screen message appears confirming your local data was saved to the new account
  7. Close the overlay and confirm the header's account icon now shows the logged-in state (tooltip: "Account (logged in)")

Login on your second device

  1. On your first device (now logged in), click the account icon to reopen the overlay
  2. Click "Add a Device (Link)"
  3. Verify the "Add a Device" modal opens with a one-time link (note: it expires soon and can only be used once)
  4. Copy the link (or use "Email Me a Link" instead) and open it on your second device
  5. On the second device, verify you land on a "Set up passkey" screen with a pre-filled device nickname
  6. Click "Set Up Passkey" and complete your device's passkey prompt
  7. Verify the page reloads and the account icon shows the logged-in state
  8. Click the statistics icon and verify the stats match the account from your first device
  9. Log out on the second device, then click "Log In" (not "Create Passkey") and verify you're signed back in using the passkey you just created

"Don't ask me to log in"

  1. On a logged-out device, when the login prompt appears on load, click "Don't ask me to log in" instead of signing up or logging in
  2. Verify the overlay closes
  3. Reload the page
  4. Verify the login prompt does NOT automatically reappear this time
  5. Click the account icon to open the overlay manually — verify the "Don't ask me to log in" link is no longer shown, but you can still create a passkey or log in from here
  6. Log in (or create a passkey) from this manual overlay
  7. Log out
  8. Reload the page again
  9. Verify the login prompt automatically reappears this time — logging in on a device resets the "don't ask" setting so a future logout doesn't leave you stranded on a signed-out device

Warn on Non-Answers (new in v1.0.4)

A legal word isn't always a possible answer — the answer list is much smaller than the list of words you're allowed to guess. This preference asks you to confirm before you spend a guess on a word that can't be the answer, starting from whichever line you choose. It's off by default.

Useful words for testing (verified against the shipped lists):

  • CRANE — legal and a possible answer, so it should never warn
  • ADIEU, SLATS, TARES — legal but not possible answers, so these should warn

Turning it on and setting the starting line

  1. Open the settings modal (gear icon in header)
  2. Find "Warn on Non-Answers, Starting Line:" and verify the toggle is off and the stepper reads 3 by default
  3. Click the stepper's up arrow and verify the number increases; hold at the top and verify it stops at 6
  4. Click the down arrow repeatedly and verify it stops at 1 (it should never go to 0 or negative)
  5. Set the starting line to 3 and turn the toggle on
  6. Close and reopen the settings modal and verify both the toggle and the number were remembered

The warning itself

  1. With the setting on and the starting line at 3, start today's puzzle
  2. On line 1, guess ADIEU — verify you are not warned, because line 1 is before the starting line
  3. On line 2, guess SLATS — verify you are still not warned
  4. On line 3, guess TARES — verify a message appears saying it's a legal word but not in the list of possible answers, with "Change my guess" and "Use anyway"
  5. Click "Change my guess" and verify the guess is not submitted — the word stays on the board for you to edit, and you have not lost a turn
  6. Guess TARES again on the same line and this time click "Use anyway"
  7. Verify the guess is submitted normally and scores as usual
  8. On the next line, guess CRANE and verify no warning appears — it's a possible answer

Starting line and off-switch behave

  1. Set the starting line to 1, then start a fresh puzzle (or use a new browser profile)
  2. Guess ADIEU on line 1 and verify you are warned immediately this time
  3. Turn the setting off, then guess another non-answer such as SLATS
  4. Verify no warning appears at all and the guess submits directly

History Import and Export (fixed in v1.0.4)

Both of these were failing for everyone before this release, so they need a real pass rather than a spot check.

  1. Open the Tools modal (wrench icon) and under "Backup & Restore" click "Export History"
  2. Try both formats if offered (JSON and CSV) and verify a file downloads without an error message
  3. Open the file and verify it contains your games, one row per puzzle
  4. Repeat the export while signed in — this path fetches from the server and was broken separately
  5. Now use "Import History" and select the file you just exported
  6. Verify it reports how many rows were valid and how many were added, with no crash and no "History import failed" message
  7. Since every puzzle already exists locally, expect rows to be flagged as duplicates rather than added — that's correct, not an error
  8. Verify your stats and history list are unchanged afterwards

Signing In on a Second Device (fixed in v1.0.4)

Previously, signing in on a device that had never been logged in showed a blank board and that device's own settings, instead of your account's game and preferences. Needs two devices or two browser profiles.

  1. On device A (signed in), play at least one guess of today's puzzle but do not finish it
  2. On device A, change a visible preference — turn on dark theme, or hard mode
  3. On device B, which has never been signed in to this account, sign in
  4. Verify the page reloads on its own after sign-in
  5. Verify today's board on device B shows the guesses you made on device A, not a blank board
  6. Verify the preference you changed on device A is now in effect on device B
  7. Open settings on device B and confirm the setting is actually shown as on, not just visually applied
  8. Reload device B once more and verify both the board and the settings persist

Erase While Signed In (fixed in v1.0.4)

  1. Sign in, then finish today's puzzle
  2. Try to erase today's game
  3. Verify you get a clear message that erase is unavailable while playing online, rather than the erase appearing to succeed
  4. Log out, and verify erase becomes available again for a locally-played game
