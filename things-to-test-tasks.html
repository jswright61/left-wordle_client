Start with a History in Your Browser / Device

If you don't have a history (or a significant history) on staging for you current browser / device, consider jumpstarting your staging history from the production site.

  1. Open Left Wordle (Production)
  2. Open the Tools modal (wrench icon in header)
  3. Under "Troubleshooting" click "Download All Settings"
  4. Open Left Wordle (Staging)
  5. This time, dismiss the login prompt without clicking "Don't ask me to log in" — we still want it to prompt you again for the Logins task below
  6. Open the Tools modal (same wrench icon, now on the left side of the header)
  7. Under "Backup & Restore" click "Restore from File"
  8. Select the file you just downloaded
  9. Click "Overwrite & Restore" to confirm
  10. Click the statistics (bar graph) icon in the header and verify the stats match your production stats
  11. Note: these stats are a point-in-time copy of production, not linked to it. Games played here will not be reflected on production.
  12. Refresh the page, or click the account icon to proceed with account testing.

Logins

You should be prompted to create a login. PLEASE create one and test various scenarios listed below. This is the biggest change yet for Left Wordle.

Logins use Passkeys — Passkey info here. Without an optional email, your login info on the server will not contain any personally identifiable information.

Signup on your first device

  1. In the login overlay, optionally enter an email address (optional — this is just for account recovery, not required)
  2. Optionally edit the device nickname field (it's pre-filled with a guessed name)
  3. Click "Create Passkey"
  4. Complete your device/browser's passkey prompt (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key)
  5. Verify the status message shows "Passkey created." and the overlay switches to the logged-in view
  6. Verify a brief on-screen message appears confirming your local data was saved to the new account
  7. Close the overlay and confirm the header's account icon now shows the logged-in state (tooltip: "Account (logged in)")

Login on your second device

  1. On your first device (now logged in), click the account icon to reopen the overlay
  2. Click "Add a Device (Link)"
  3. Verify the "Add a Device" modal opens with a one-time link (note: it expires soon and can only be used once)
  4. Copy the link (or use "Email Me a Link" instead) and open it on your second device
  5. On the second device, verify you land on a "Set up passkey" screen with a pre-filled device nickname
  6. Click "Set Up Passkey" and complete your device's passkey prompt
  7. Verify the page reloads and the account icon shows the logged-in state
  8. Click the statistics icon and verify the stats match the account from your first device
  9. Log out on the second device, then click "Log In" (not "Create Passkey") and verify you're signed back in using the passkey you just created

"Don't ask me to log in"

  1. On a logged-out device, when the login prompt appears on load, click "Don't ask me to log in" instead of signing up or logging in
  2. Verify the overlay closes
  3. Reload the page
  4. Verify the login prompt does NOT automatically reappear this time
  5. Click the account icon to open the overlay manually — verify the "Don't ask me to log in" link is no longer shown, but you can still create a passkey or log in from here
  6. Log in (or create a passkey) from this manual overlay
  7. Log out
  8. Reload the page again
  9. Verify the login prompt automatically reappears this time — logging in on a device resets the "don't ask" setting so a future logout doesn't leave you stranded on a signed-out device

Everyday Play Still Works (regression — v1.0.5)

Every guess and completion now sends a little more information to the server than it used to. Nothing about that should be visible, so this is a plain "did we break playing the game" pass. Worth doing first, and worth doing both signed out and signed in.

  1. Signed out, play today's puzzle to a finish (win or lose)
  2. Verify guesses score normally, the board reveals as usual, and the result modal appears
  3. Verify your stats and streak updated the way you'd expect
  4. Reload the page and verify the finished game is still shown, not a blank board
  5. Now sign in and repeat on another puzzle (or another device/profile)
  6. Verify the same: normal scoring, normal finish, stats updated, survives a reload
  7. Watch for any moment where the board stops accepting letters and needs a reload — that's the failure worth reporting

Finishing a Game After You Get Signed Out (new in v1.0.5)

If your session ends part-way through a puzzle, you drop back to playing on the device alone. Before this release, the game you then finished stayed on that device forever — it never reached your history or stats. Now it gets added the next time you sign in.

To end a session on purpose, delete the login cookie in your browser's developer tools: Application (or Storage) → Cookies → the staging site → delete left_wordle_session. Do not use Log Out — logging out deliberately is a different path and won't exercise this.

  1. Sign in, start today's puzzle, and make one or two guesses
  2. Delete the left_wordle_session cookie as above (leave the tab open)
  3. Make another guess — verify you're told your session ended and you're now playing offline, rather than the board freezing or silently swallowing the guess
  4. Finish the puzzle
  5. Sign back in
  6. Verify the game you just finished is in your history, with the right result
  7. Verify your stats reflect it — games played went up, and a win counted as a win
  8. Reload once and verify it's still there and hasn't been counted twice

The Same Puzzle on Two Devices (regression — v1.0.5)

Behind the scenes this release changes how a game played on more than one device gets recorded. You shouldn't be able to tell — this is a check that nothing about the visible behaviour moved. Needs two devices or two browser profiles, both signed in to the same account.

  1. On device A, start today's puzzle and make a guess or two, but don't finish
  2. On device B, open the same puzzle and finish it
  3. Verify the win or loss counts once in your stats — not twice, and not zero times
  4. Verify your streak moved the way it should
  5. Check your history and verify today appears once, with the finished result
  6. Go back to device A, reload, and verify it agrees — same result, same stats

Backup and Restore While Signed In (regression — v1.0.5)

The data written into a history export changed slightly this release, so a round trip is worth re-checking even though nothing about it should look different.

  1. Signed in, open the Tools modal (wrench icon) and export your history
  2. Verify the file downloads and contains your games, one row per puzzle
  3. Import that same file back
  4. Verify it reports what it found without an error, and that already-known puzzles are flagged as duplicates rather than added again
  5. Verify your stats and streak are unchanged afterwards — an import should never move your numbers on its own

Reading Game Data From the New Store (staging flag — Phase 3)

Staging can be switched to read your history and stats from the new per-account store instead of the old per-device one. Nothing about the app should look different — that's the entire point, and the only way to find out is to look hard at a real account with real history.

Ask for the flag to be turned on for staging before working through this (it's a server setting, not something in the app), and note roughly when it was switched so anything odd can be tied to it.

Before the flag is turned on — capture what "correct" looks like, so you have something to compare against:

  1. Sign in on staging and open your statistics — note games played, win %, current and max streak, and the shape of the guess distribution
  2. Open your history and note how many puzzles are listed and the most recent few results
  3. Export a backup and keep the file

After the flag is turned on:

  1. Reload and open your statistics again — every number should be identical. Any change at all is worth reporting, however small
  2. Open your history — same puzzle count, same results, nothing missing and nothing new
  3. Play today's puzzle to a finish and verify stats move exactly as they should — games played up by one, streak behaving
  4. Export a backup again and compare it against the earlier file; the games should match
  5. Import that backup and verify it reports duplicates rather than adding anything, and that your stats do not move

The two-device case, which is what this whole change is for. Needs two devices or profiles signed in to the same account:

  1. On device A, start a puzzle and make a guess or two without finishing
  2. On device B, finish that same puzzle
  3. Verify the result counts once — not twice, not zero times
  4. Reload device A and verify it agrees with device B about the result
  5. Check your history shows that puzzle once, with the finished result

If anything here looks wrong, say so before the flag goes anywhere near production — it can be switched back off without a deploy.
